Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

City of Windows does not collect any personal data.

  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • The app makes no network requests. It works fully offline.
  • Your progress, settings and tunes are stored only on your device, using the app's local storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to copy and send a tune code yourself.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.
  • The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, export or delete from us. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

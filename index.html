I can't hear anything

Turn your volume up and tap any window. City of Windows plays sound even when the silent switch is on. If you use Bluetooth headphones, check they're connected.

How do I solve a stop?

Press Listen to hear the tune. The street lamps flash on each beat, which shows you which column each note is in. Lower floors play lower notes. Light the windows you think match, then press Check: right windows glow gold, wrong ones flash red, and a lamp marks steps that still need a note.

How do I share a tune?

In Free play, press Tune code and send the code to a friend. They paste it in Free play to hear exactly what you made.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. If you delete the app, your stars and saved tunes are deleted too.
